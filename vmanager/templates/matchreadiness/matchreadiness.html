How the readiness score works

The Match Readiness Score uses a machine learning model that reviews multiple player signals to give coaches a clearer view of match preparedness.

✓
Attendance History
Recent participation patterns help the model understand consistency, availability, and match preparation.
✦
Recent Performance
Match records such as production, efficiency, and positive actions help reflect current form and impact.
✚
Health Context
Injury status and recovery context help keep the final recommendation grounded in player safety.
